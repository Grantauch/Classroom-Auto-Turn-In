Security, privacy & free-tier limits

Only the missing assignment details, your planning instructions, and correction notes are sent to the selected service. Groq says ordinary inference prompts are not retained by default. Do not include student-specific personal information.

It does not automatically read your other Classroom lessons, Drive files, or student work. Free limits can change or temporarily fill up. If every connected free service is unavailable, AI drafting stops safely. Normal Auto Turn-In keeps working.
